Data stored on your device

Game progress, unlocked and selected Brainballs, settings, scores, streaks, language, audio preferences, and the age category are stored locally. MoonSol Studios cannot remotely access or delete this local data.

To delete it, open Android Settings > Apps > Brainrot Chaos: Kavvoro > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the Game. Android backup may restore eligible data if backup is enabled; backup data can be managed through your Google account and device backup settings.

Google Play Games, advertising, and purchases

Google controls data processed through Google Play Games, AdMob, Google Play Billing, consent services, and eligible Android backup. This can include Play Games profile and leaderboard activity, advertising identifiers and interactions, diagnostics, purchase ownership, and transaction records.

  • Delete Play Games data for this Game from your Play Games profile and data controls.
  • Manage advertising privacy through the Game's Privacy menu and your Google Account data and privacy controls.
  • Manage purchases and payment activity through Google Play order history and Google account controls.

MoonSol Studios cannot directly delete records controlled by Google. Google may retain purchase, security, fraud-prevention, or legal records according to its policies and applicable law.

What MoonSol Studios deletes or retains

When a valid request concerns data controlled by MoonSol Studios, we delete the identified support correspondence and related request records after the request is completed, normally within 30 days. We may retain only information reasonably required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or proof that the request was fulfilled, for as long as that purpose requires.

The Game does not operate a MoonSol Studios account system, a separate cloud-save database, or a separate leaderboard database. Therefore, there is no MoonSol Studios game account to delete.
