3. Information stored locally

Age category

Each Game asks for an age in years and immediately maps it in memory to one of three broad categories: child, teen or adult. The numeric age is discarded. Only the category is stored on the device and used to configure advertising restrictions, consent and age-appropriate behavior. Resetting game progress does not reset this privacy category.

Game data and preferences

Progress, unlocked content, scores, game economy, objectives, settings, language, audio, accessibility choices and related play state are stored locally. Android backup may restore eligible local data when device backup is enabled. MoonSol Studios does not receive this local save through its own servers.

4. Advertising and consent in both Games

The Games use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on age category, region, consent and device settings, Google may collect or share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app launches, taps, ad impressions, video views and other product interactions;
  • diagnostic and performance information; and
  • device or account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID or app set ID when permitted.

This information is used by Google for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit.

The Games apply restricted treatment and age-appropriate maximum ad ratings for child and teen categories. Advertising does not initialize until the age category exists and UMP reports that ads may be requested. Where UMP requires a privacy choice, the form is shown and can later be reopened from the Game’s privacy menu.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy, Google’s explanation of information from apps that use Google services, and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

5. Firebase Analytics in Rift Lab

Rift Lab uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand whether onboarding, progression and rewarded-ad features work as intended. Analytics collection is disabled at Android startup and is enabled only after the neutral age screen has produced a category. Rift Lab disables Analytics collection of the Android Advertising ID and marks Analytics data as unavailable for ads personalization.

Firebase Analytics may process a randomly generated app-instance identifier, masked-IP-derived general location, device and app information, app lifecycle events and the following gameplay measurements:

  • tutorial step and completion state;
  • first merge and first special discovery;
  • prestige/Surge and sector-unlock progress;
  • session duration and aggregate actions during a session;
  • use of the stranded-item scrap tool; and
  • whether each rewarded ad was shown and completed, identified only by placement.

These events do not contain a name, email address, exact age, advertising identifier, precise location, contacts or the contents of the local save. MoonSol Studios uses the resulting aggregate reports for product analytics and reliability decisions, not to build directly identified player profiles.

Learn more from Firebase Privacy and Security and Google Analytics data safeguards.

6. Additional Google services in Kavvoro

Google Play Games

If Play Games features are available and used, Google processes the player’s Play Games profile and submitted game activity. Kavvoro may submit eligible scores to Google-hosted leaderboards. MoonSol Studios does not operate a separate leaderboard database.

Google Play purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment credentials. Kavvoro receives product ownership and transaction status needed to unlock, acknowledge and restore an item; MoonSol Studios does not receive full card or bank-account details.

Replay sharing

Replay videos are generated on the device and leave the Game only when the player chooses Share and selects another app. The selected service then processes the video under its own policy.

7. Why information is used

  • to provide gameplay, local saves, settings, purchases and optional platform features;
  • to show, measure, limit and secure advertising where permitted;
  • to measure onboarding, retention and feature reliability in Rift Lab;
  • to prevent invalid traffic, abuse and fraud; and
  • to comply with consent, age-related, legal and safety requirements.

8. Sharing, sale and third parties

MoonSol Studios does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the Google services described above to provide advertising, consent, analytics, purchases, leaderboards, security, diagnostics and related platform functionality. A replay is disclosed to another service only when the player explicitly initiates sharing.

Service providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. MoonSol Studios does not authorize them to use information outside the purposes described by their applicable services and settings.

9. Retention and deletion

Local Game data remains until it is cleared, overwritten, restored from an eligible Android backup or the Game is uninstalled. The Games do not create a MoonSol Studios account.

Google-controlled advertising, consent, analytics, purchase and Play Games information is retained according to Google’s service settings, legal obligations and policies. Clearing Rift Lab’s storage or uninstalling it removes local data and causes a later installation to use a new Analytics app-instance identifier. Aggregate reports may remain without a direct identifier.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, keep necessary business records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse or comply with law. Read our Data Deletion page for instructions.

10. Security and international processing

Network communication with Google services uses encrypted connections supplied by their SDKs. Local saves are protected by the security of the player’s device and Android environment. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Google may process information in countries other than the player’s country, subject to its contractual and legal safeguards.

11. Choices and privacy rights

  • Open Ad Privacy Options in a Game when the option is required and available.
  • Manage the Android Advertising ID and privacy controls in Android Settings.
  • Clear the Game’s storage or uninstall it to remove local progress and reset local identifiers.
  • Manage Play Games, purchase and Google activity through the relevant Google Account controls.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection where applicable.

Because the Games do not ask for a MoonSol Studios account and Rift Lab Analytics is pseudonymous, we may be unable to connect an email request to a particular anonymous Analytics instance without additional information that the Game does not currently expose.

12. Children and teens

The Games use a neutral age screen and do not ask players to provide a name, email address, precise location, contacts or exact date of birth inside the Game. Child and teen categories receive restricted advertising configuration. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions.

13. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when a Game, its SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated before or when the related release becomes available.
