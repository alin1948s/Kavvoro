Privacy Policy

Brainrot Chaos: Kavvoro

Effective date: July 10, 2026.
This policy explains how Brainrot Chaos: Kavvoro (the “Game”), published by MoonSol Studios, handles information when you play, view ads, use Google Play Games, make a purchase, or share a replay.

1. Who we are

MoonSol Studios is the developer and publisher of Brainrot Chaos: Kavvoro, Android package com.moonsolstudios.kavvoro. Contact us at moonsolstudios@gmail.com.

2. Information handled by the Game

Age category

The Game asks for an age in years and immediately converts it into one of three categories: child, teen, or adult. The exact age is not retained. Only the resulting age category is stored locally on the device and used to configure age-appropriate advertising restrictions and consent handling.

Local game data

Progress, unlocked and selected Brainballs, settings, scores, streaks, language, audio preferences, and the age category are stored locally. Android backup may restore eligible local data when device backup is enabled.

Advertising and consent

The Game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Depending on age, region, consent, and device settings, Google may process device or other identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions, app interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. Child and teen profiles receive restricted treatment configured by the Game. Ads are requested only when Google’s consent system reports that ads may be requested.

Google Play Games

If Google Play Games is available, Google processes your Play Games profile and game activity. The Game submits highest levels and longest streaks to the four global leaderboards. MoonSol Studios does not operate a separate leaderboard database.

Purchases

Optional Brainball purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details. The Game receives product ownership and transaction status needed to unlock, acknowledge, and restore purchases. MoonSol Studios does not receive full card or bank account details.

Replay sharing

Replay videos are generated on the device. They are shared only when you choose Share and select another app. Once shared, the selected service processes the video under its own privacy policy.

Analytics

Firebase Analytics collection is disabled in the current release of the Game. If analytics is enabled in a future update, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that release.

3. Why information is used

  • to provide gameplay, saved local progress, settings, purchases, and leaderboards;
  • to show, measure, limit, and secure advertising where permitted;
  • to comply with consent, age-related, legal, safety, and fraud-prevention requirements;
  • to diagnose failures and maintain the security and reliability of Google-powered services.

4. Service providers

The Game relies on Google services, including Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads, User Messaging Platform, and eligible Android backup services. Their processing is governed by Google’s terms and privacy policies. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Children and teens

The Game uses a neutral age screen to configure age-appropriate ad treatment. MoonSol Studios does not ask players to provide a name, email address, precise location, contacts, or exact date of birth inside the Game. We do not knowingly operate our own server-side profiles for children. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions.

6. Data sharing and sale

MoonSol Studios does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the Google services listed above to provide advertising, purchases, leaderboards, consent, security, diagnostics, and related platform functionality. A replay is disclosed to another service only when the player explicitly initiates sharing.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until it is cleared, overwritten, restored from an eligible Android backup, or the Game is uninstalled. The Game does not create a separate MoonSol Studios account. Google Play profile, purchase, advertising, and leaderboard information is retained and controlled by Google according to its policies and account controls.

You can clear local data in Android Settings, uninstall the Game, adjust ad privacy choices from the Privacy menu, and manage Google activity through your Google Account. You may also use our dedicated Data Deletion page to submit a request. We will respond to requests concerning data controlled by MoonSol Studios and direct requests concerning Google-controlled data to the relevant Google controls.

8. Security and international processing

Network communication with Google services uses encrypted connections provided by their SDKs. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Google may process information in countries other than the player’s country, subject to its legal safeguards.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. You may withdraw eligible advertising consent through the in-game Privacy menu. Contact moonsolstudios@gmail.com to exercise rights concerning information controlled by MoonSol Studios.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game, its SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will be reflected before or when the related Game update is released.
