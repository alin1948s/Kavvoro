# Brainrot Chaos: Kavvoro

## Short description

Tap the Rift in a fast physics arcade with 50 Brainballs and endless chaos.

## Full description

THE RIFT DOES NOT FOLLOW RULES. IT FOLLOWS YOUR FINGER.

Brainrot Chaos: Kavvoro is a fast one-touch physics arcade game where every tap bends a volatile energy tether. Pull your Brainball through moving platforms, wind fields, pulse zones, portals and tiny exits before the Rift collapses.

Easy to touch. Hard to master. Very difficult to put down.

TWO ENDLESS MODES

CLASSIC builds precision one hazard at a time. Learn the rhythm, protect your Rift Energy and chase cleaner runs through an endless stream of increasingly complex levels.

CHAOS remixes the rules with wilder layouts, aggressive modifiers and a separate level system. It is unpredictable, but every run remains readable and skill-based.

50 BRAINBALLS TO COLLECT

Meet Kav Zero, Mimi Voro, King Kav and dozens of other original mutations. Every Brainball has its own look, effects, personality and voice line. Reach higher levels, build longer streaks, earn Hype and complete challenges to unlock the entire vault.

Some rare Brainballs carry real superpowers: phase through danger, conserve Rift Energy, rebound harder or trigger a powerful surge. Choose the mutation that fits your run.

BUILT FOR FAST RUNS

- Responsive one-finger Rift control
- Ten guided training levels for each mode
- Infinite Classic and Chaos progression
- Portals, wind, moving walls, pulse fields and changing hazards
- Hype rewards, streak vaults and a Daily Rift bonus
- Global leaderboards for highest level and longest streak
- Vertical replay videos made for Shorts, Reels and TikTok
- Original music, dynamic effects and character voices
- Optional rewarded continues and restorable premium Brainballs

Every clean dodge builds Chain. Every efficient move earns more Hype. Every failed run teaches you exactly where the Rift snapped.

Can you control the chaos, or will Kavvoro control you?

Brainrot Chaos: Kavvoro is an original universe by MoonSol Studios.
